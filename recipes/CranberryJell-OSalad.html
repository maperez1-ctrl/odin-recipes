Cranberry Jell-O Salad

Home

Description

Cranberry Jello salad is a sweet and tangy chilled side dish made with cranberry sauce, fruit, and raspberry or cherry gelatin. It has a refreshing texture and is a colorful favorite for holiday meals.

Ingredients

  • Water: Before you start combining the ingredients, you'll bring two cups of water to a boil.
  • Gelatin mix: You'll need two packages of cherry-flavored gelatin mix (such as Jell-O).
  • Cranberry sauce: Opt for canned, jellied cranberry sauce.
  • Pineapple: A can of crushed pineapple adds even more bright, fruity flavor to this cranberry Jell-O salad.
  • Nuts: It’s finished with a sprinkle of nuts for crunch. The recipe calls for chopped pecans, but you can use walnuts if you prefer.

steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine boiling water and gelatin mix in a bowl; stir until gelatin is dissolved.
  3. Stir in cranberry sauce and crushed pineapple.
  4. Transfer to a large glass serving dish or 12 individual dishes. Sprinkle pecans on top.
  5. Chill until set, 3 to 4 hours for individual dishes, or 4 hours to overnight for one large serving dish.
