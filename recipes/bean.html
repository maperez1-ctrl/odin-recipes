Description

Baked beans are tender beans slow-cooked in a rich, savory-sweet sauce, often made with tomato, brown sugar, and seasonings. They’re hearty, flavorful, and perfect as a comforting side dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 (29 ounce) can baked beans with pork
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce

steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C)..
  2. Combine baked beans, brown sugar, ketchup, and Worcestershire sauce in a casserole dish.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven, covered, until hot and bubbly, about 45 minutes.
