Description

Steamed hard-boiled eggs are eggs cooked gently over boiling water until the whites are firm and the yolks are fully set. Steaming makes them easy to peel and gives them a smooth, tender texture.

Ingredients

  • 12 eggs, at room temperature

steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Place a steamer insert into a pot and fill with water to just below the bottom of the steamer. Bring water to just below a boil over medium heat. Gently place the eggs in the steamer insert, cover the pot, and steam for 15 minutes.
  3. Immediately transfer eggs to a bowl of ice water until cool enough to handle.
  4. Make a small crack on the large end of each egg and place eggs back into the ice water for about 20 minutes, then peel.
